📖 Overview

▼

This guide covers the complete achievement system for CFCMonitor, showing you all the different ways achievements can be created and what makes each type special.

Whether you want to suggest an achievement idea or understand what's possible, this guide will show you the creative potential of each criteria type.

Achievement Ideas Welcome! Use this guide to understand what each criteria type can do, then suggest your own creative achievements using these building blocks.

🎯 Basic Achievement Criteria Types

▼

These achievements reward players for reaching milestones in combat and survival. Perfect for recognizing dedication and progress over time.

TOTAL KILLS

What it's for: Celebrating combat prowess against other players
Achievement ideas: "First Blood" (1 kill), "Veteran Fighter" (50 kills), "Combat Legend" (500 kills)
Creative uses:

  • Create progression tiers from rookie to elite fighter
  • Reward players for participating in PvP combat
  • Build reputation systems around combat achievements
  • Tip: Works great for org recruitment - shows active PvP participation

TOTAL DEATHS

What it's for: Adding humor to defeats and encouraging resilience
Achievement ideas: "Learning Experience" (1 death), "Battle Scarred" (25 deaths), "Phoenix Rising" (100 deaths)
Creative uses:

  • Turn negative experiences into positive progression
  • Create funny "badges of dishonor" that players can laugh about
  • Reward persistence and willingness to engage in risky PvP
  • Tip: These achievements help reduce PvP anxiety by making deaths feel less punishing

TOTAL PVE KILLS

What it's for: Recognizing PvE combat mastery and mission completion
Achievement ideas: "Bounty Hunter" (100 NPC kills), "Sector Cleaner" (1000 kills), "AI Nemesis" (5000 kills)
Creative uses:

  • Reward players who focus on PvE content like bunker missions
  • Create milestones for different types of gameplay preferences
  • Recognize dedication to clearing security forces or pirate threats
  • Tip: Great for players who prefer cooperative or solo PvE over PvP combat

TOTAL PVE DEATHS

What it's for: Acknowledging the learning curve and exploration risks
Achievement ideas: "Security Encounter" (first death to guards), "Underestimated the Opposition" (10 NPC deaths), "Lesson Learned" (50 deaths)
Creative uses:

  • Mark first encounters with different types of threats
  • Create educational achievements about game world dangers
  • Reward exploration of high-risk areas with tough NPCs
  • Tip: Perfect for new player guidance - shows they're exploring and learning

🔫 Weapon-Specific Achievements

▼

These achievements celebrate mastery with specific weapons and can create interesting social dynamics around equipment choices.

PVP SPECIFIC KILL WEAPON

What it's for: Rewarding skilled use of particular weapons against players
Achievement ideas: "Sniper Elite" (kill with sniper rifle), "Close Quarters" (kill with shotgun), "Old School" (kill with basic pistol)
Creative uses:

  • Create weapon mastery challenges that encourage trying different loadouts
  • Build prestige around difficult-to-use or rare weapons
  • Design themed achievements around weapon categories
  • Tip: Great for encouraging weapon diversity in PvP encounters

PVE SPECIFIC KILL WEAPON

What it's for: Testing weapon effectiveness against NPCs in a safer environment
Achievement ideas: "Field Test Complete" (kill NPC with new weapon), "Bunker Buster" (clear facility with specific weapon), "Pirate Hunter" (eliminate pirates with chosen gun)
Creative uses:

  • Perfect for weapon tutorial achievements - easier than PvP requirements
  • Create mission-specific weapon challenges
  • Build achievements around roleplay scenarios (bounty hunter, security contractor)
  • Tip: Ideal stepping stone before attempting similar PvP weapon achievements

PVP SPECIFIC DEATH WEAPON

What it's for: Adding humor to defeats and creating memorable "war stories"
Achievement ideas: "Humbled by a Pistol" (killed by basic sidearm), "Railgun Victim" (hit by sniper), "Explosives Expert Target" (killed by grenades)
Creative uses:

  • Turn embarrassing deaths into funny achievements players can share
  • Create social dynamics where players hunt specific weapon kills
  • Build achievements around experiencing different combat styles
  • Tip: These can actually make players less frustrated about dying in PvP

PVE SPECIFIC DEATH WEAPON

What it's for: Warning about area-specific threats and first-time exploration
Achievement ideas: "Security Checkpoint" (killed by station turrets), "Underestimated Security" (killed by guard's rifle), "Bunker Hazard" (killed by facility defenses)
Creative uses:

  • Mark first encounters with dangerous areas or security systems
  • Create exploration achievements tied to specific locations
  • Build educational achievements about environmental dangers
  • Tip: Excellent for teaching new players about different threat levels

⚡ Event-Specific Achievements

▼

These achievements capture special moments and unusual circumstances that make for memorable gameplay stories.

CRASH EVENT

What it's for: Acknowledging ship crashes and tracking piloting incidents
Achievement ideas: "First Crash" (any ship crash), "Crash Test Dummy" (multiple crashes), "Aurora Wrecker" (crash in Aurora), "Connie Down" (crash in Constellation)
Creative uses:

  • Create general crash achievements for new pilot experiences
  • Build progression achievements for frequent crashers
  • Design ship-specific crash achievements for different vessels
  • Tip: Can track crashes by ship type - great for ship-specific humor achievements

SUICIDE EVENT

What it's for: Detecting when suicide is the cause of death
Achievement ideas: "Self-Inflicted" (first suicide damage), "Oops Moment" (accidentally killed yourself), "Intentional Exit" (deliberate self-termination)
Creative uses:

  • Create achievements for accidental self-harm incidents
  • Track when players accidentally hurt themselves with their own weapons
  • Build achievements around learning weapon safety the hard way
  • Tip: This tracks suicide as a damage type - also counts toward total death counter

SELF DESTRUCT EVENT

What it's for: Recognizing dramatic ship sacrifices and last-stand moments
Achievement ideas: "Going Down with the Ship" (first self-destruct), "Scorched Earth" (deny enemies your vessel), "Captain's Choice" (heroic self-sacrifice)
Creative uses:

  • Create cinematic achievements around dramatic ship destruction
  • Build achievements around preventing ship capture
  • Design achievements for tactical denial of resources to enemies
  • Tip: Perfect for creating memorable "last stand" moments in PvP

SUFFOCATION DEATH EVENT

What it's for: Teaching about environmental dangers and EVA safety
Achievement ideas: "Vacuum Lesson" (first suffocation), "EVA Gone Wrong" (died during spacewalk), "Life Support Critical" (oxygen system failure)
Creative uses:

  • Create educational achievements about space survival
  • Build achievements around exploration risks and safety learning
  • Design achievements that teach players about life support systems
  • Tip: Perfect for new player education about environmental hazards

📚 What You Can Use in Achievement Suggestions

▼

When suggesting achievements, you can reference these specific game elements. Just mention them by name in your suggestion - we'll handle the technical details!

Ways People Can Die or Kill

Your achievement suggestions can involve these different types of combat and death scenarios:

  • takedown - Melee kills/deaths
  • collision - Impact/crash damage
  • suicide - Self-inflicted damage
  • vehicle destruction - Vehicle destruction events
  • selfdestruct - Self-destruct events
  • suffocation - Oxygen deprivation deaths
  • bleed out - Bleeding out deaths
  • bullet - Ballistic projectile damage
  • explosion - Explosive damage
  • normal - Environmental damage
  • punish - Punishment system damage
  • blunt force - Blunt force trauma damage
  • hazard - Environmental hazards
  • electric arc - Electric damage
  • BoundaryViolation - Flying outside boundary in AC

Tips for Suggesting Achievements

  • Be Creative! You can suggest achievements involving any weapons, ships, or locations you've seen in Star Citizen
  • Use Recognizable Names - Reference ships like "Aurora," "Constellation," or locations like "Lorville" or "Area18"
  • Mix and Match - Combine different criteria types for unique achievements (like "kill with a specific weapon at a specific location")
  • Don't Worry About Details - Just describe your idea clearly - we'll figure out the technical implementation
  • Think About Stories - The best achievements create memorable moments players will want to share

💡 Example Suggestion Format:

"I'd like an achievement called 'Aurora Pilot's Nightmare' for crashing an Aurora ship. Maybe something funny like 'Close Quarters Specialist' for getting 5 takedown kills. Or 'Lorville Tourist' for dying at Lorville!"

See? Just describe what you want - we handle the rest!
